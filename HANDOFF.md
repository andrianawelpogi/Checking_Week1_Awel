# UX Project Proposal: Auto Repair Shop Management System

## 1. User Problem Statement
Small-to-medium motorcycle and automotive repair shops frequently struggle with disorganized paper logs, leading to miscommunication on repair turnaround times, unclear service pricing, and lost customer service records.

## 2. Target Audience
* **Primary Users:** Car repair shop managers, motorcycle service centers, independent mechanics, and vehicle owners tracking live queue statuses.

## 3. Chosen Aesthetic Theme
* **Industrial Professional:** Features a high-contrast dark slate navigation header (`#1e293b`), amber operational accent markers (`#d97706`), and clean modular layout cards optimized for high visibility in busy workshop environments.

## 4. Multi-Page Architecture Overview
* `index.html`: Home page highlighting shop background and facility capabilities.
* `product.html`: Visual catalog displaying spare parts for sale with explicit prices.
* `services.html`: Flat-rate breakdown of maintenance operations and service fees.
* `currentRepair.html`: Interactive queue portal allowing users to register tickets into the active queue, update service statuses (`In Service`, `Done`, `Queueing`), and automatically archive finished records into the permanent Customer Log table.
